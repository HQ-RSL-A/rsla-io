## What an AI automation agency does

We find the work you do every day that a machine should be doing, and we build the machine. Not a chatbot bolted onto your homepage. The small stuff: the lead that texted at 9pm and got no reply until Tuesday, the invoice reminder you forgot, the review request you meant to send, the same intake questions typed into three systems.

Each of those is an automation waiting to be built. Most of them take a week or two. Some take an afternoon.

## What we automate most often

**Lead response.** It comes first, because speed decides who gets the job. A missed call gets a text back in seconds. A form submission gets a reply that references what the person asked, not "we got your message." Follow up runs on a schedule until they book or say no.

**The admin around the work.** Appointment reminders and rescheduling, intake that fills your CRM without you typing, invoices and payment nudges, review requests after every job, the weekly numbers pulled into one dashboard instead of five tabs.

**AI agents.** An assistant that answers the phone when you cannot. A bot that turns a voice note into a task list or a new lead. A pipeline that turns one recording into a month of content. Our [client intake guide](/blog/automate-client-intake-ai) walks through one of these end to end.

## What it looks like by business type

**Dental office.** Recall reminders that rebook the six month cleaning without the front desk calling.

**HVAC company.** Missed call text back and a maintenance plan reminder that goes out before summer.

**Property manager.** Maintenance requests logged, assigned, and confirmed to the tenant without a phone call.

**Coaching business.** A discovery call funnel that qualifies before it books.

**Nonprofit.** Volunteer signups onboarded, tagged, and messaged without a coordinator.

Same idea every time: find the task you repeat, decide the rule, hand it to software, keep the judgment calls for yourself.

## What you get, and what you do not

Automation does not bring in customers. It keeps the ones who already called and gives back the hours the admin was taking. Follow up that runs itself means no lead waits until Tuesday; intake that fills itself means no data entry at 11pm. That is the whole promise for this service. What you do with the hours is yours to decide.

## How we build it

**Diagnosis first.** Which automation is worth building depends on where your hours go, so the first call is us asking and you talking.

**Where we start.** We rank every task on the list by three things: hours a week, how often it goes wrong, and what it costs when it does. The top of that list is the first build, and it is usually lead response, because a lead that waits a day is a lead somebody else answered. The second build is whichever admin task you are still doing after dinner.

**Built around your tools.** Your calendar, your inbox, your CRM, your accounting software. The work runs in [n8n](https://n8n.io) or [Make](https://www.make.com) for workflows, GoHighLevel for anything that touches leads and follow up, and AI agents where a task needs judgment instead of rules. If the CRM is the missing piece, we set that up too; see [CRM systems](/services/crm). If nothing off the shelf fits, we write the software; see [custom builds](/services/custom-builds).

**Documented and handed over.** We document it, train you on it, and either hand it over or keep it running for you. Automations need maintenance because the apps they connect to change. A retainer covers that. Managing it yourself is fine too.

## What we need from you

Logins for the tools the automation touches, or a shared screen while we connect them. One walk through of the task as you do it today, out loud, mistakes included, because the mistakes are where the rules hide. A few real examples: three recent leads, three recent invoices, whatever the automation will handle. And an hour for testing with your real data before it goes live. If a login has to be shared, we set up a separate user for us, and you remove it the day the build is handed over.

## Questions people ask about AI automation

### How much do AI automation agencies charge?

A single automation, like missed call text back or an intake flow, is a fixed project price. A system that connects several tools with AI in the middle is a larger project, and keeping it running is a monthly retainer. We scope it on the first call and give you one number. The comparison that matters is against the hours it replaces, and we put that math in the plan.

### What kinds of work can you automate?

Lead response, follow up, booking and reminders, intake, invoicing and payment nudges, review requests, reporting, content repurposing, and internal handoffs between tools. If it needs a judgment call every time, it stays with you. If it needs the same decision every time, it goes on the list.

### Which tools do you use?

n8n and Make for workflows, GoHighLevel where leads and follow up are involved, and AI agents for the steps that need judgment. We pick the tool that fits the job and your existing stack, and we do not make you switch software to get the automation.

### Do I need to maintain it myself?

No. Everything is documented and handed over, and we offer a retainer for monitoring, fixes, and the next build. If you want to run it yourself, we train you before we leave.
