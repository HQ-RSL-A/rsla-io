## Questions Bakersfield owners ask about SEO

### What does an SEO company actually do?

Two jobs. It gets your Google Business Profile ranking on the map by fixing categories, services, reviews, photos, and hours. And it gets your website ranking in the regular results by building a page for each service, in your city, that answers the searcher's question better than the pages currently there. Then it measures both and tells you the truth about what moved.

### How much does SEO cost in Bakersfield?

A monthly retainer sized to how far you are from the pack and how many service pages you are missing. A profile that is close needs a light plan. A business with no profile, no pages, and no listings needs all three built before anything can rank, and the retainer reflects that for the first quarter. The number comes after the first call, and the scan that shows your starting point costs you nothing.

### How long until I see results?

The profile can move within days of the category and services being fixed. Reviews need two to three months to add up. A new service page on the site needs three to six months. So the order of what you will see is: the map first, the calls second, the website rankings third, and we report each one when it actually happens rather than when it is convenient.

### Is SEO dead now that people use AI?

No. The assistants take their recommendations from whatever already ranks on Google, so the Bakersfield businesses ChatGPT names are the ones already on page one.
