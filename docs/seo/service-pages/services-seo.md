## What SEO is in 2026

Search engine optimization used to mean pleasing an algorithm. It now means answering real customer questions with real expertise, one page at a time. Google's own guidance says the same thing in more words: publish [helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) written for people.

The AI assistants changed less than people think. ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews build their answers from the pages that already rank. Rank on Google and the citations follow. So the ranking work is the AI work. We do not sell them as two separate things. If your customers are local, our guide to [showing up in ChatGPT as a local business](/blog/aeo-for-local-businesses) covers the specifics.

## What our SEO services include

We start with the money pages. One page per service you actually sell, each targeting one keyword, each written to match or beat the three pages currently ranking for it. If a page for the thing you sell does not exist, nothing else matters yet. Our [websites](/services/websites) are built with this structure from day one.

Then on page SEO: titles, headings, internal links with real anchor text, image names, and an answer at the top of every page in plain language, because that is what both Google and the AI assistants extract.

Then the ongoing work. One genuinely useful article a week, built from questions your customers already ask, wired into the pages that make money. Consistency is what wins. Almost nobody keeps it up, which is exactly why it works.

Technical SEO runs underneath all of it: crawlability, indexing, speed, schema, redirects. We fix the things that tell Google to look elsewhere, and we stop at 90 percent site health instead of chasing every flag a tool throws.

## How a keyword gets chosen

Search Console first. It shows which queries already land on your site and which of them land on the wrong page, and that gap is usually the next money page. Then real search volume, from Google's own advertising data rather than a third party estimate, when a choice is close. Then the live top ten, read by a person, because the volume tools are wrong about intent more often than they are right. A term that looks commercial can turn out to be job listings and university guides when you look.

Only then does a keyword get a page. One keyword, one page, and never two pages chasing the same intent, because Google will rank neither.

## Where the weekly article goes

Each article answers one question a real customer asked, in your words, from your experience. It links to the service page it supports, and three older articles get a link back to it, because that is how a new page gets noticed without waiting months. Over a year that is roughly fifty pages of answers, all pointing at the pages that sell. That is the snowball, and it is the whole method.

## Local and programmatic

If your customers are in one city, the work shifts toward Google Maps, your Business Profile, reviews, and a page per service in that city. That is [local SEO](/services/seo/local), and it has its own page because it has its own rules. If you are in Kern County, we do it in person; see [SEO in Bakersfield](/bakersfield/seo).

If you have data that could fill hundreds of useful pages, one per product, region, or comparison, that is [programmatic SEO](/services/seo/programmatic). We build those page sets with a hard rule: every page has to carry something no other page does, or it does not get built.

## How we think about it

Never touch a page that already ranks in the top three. Match what ranks, never a fixed word count. One keyword per page, always. Fix the thin page before adding ten more. These rules are unglamorous, and they are the difference between a site that climbs and one that gets buried by the next core update.

## Who this is not for

A business that needs leads this month should run ads and come back to SEO once the cash flow allows both; SEO takes months, and a client who runs out of money in month five never sees month twelve. A national online store with ten thousand products needs an ecommerce specialist. A company that wants a report every week instead of a page every week is going to be disappointed. We say this on the first call so nobody wastes a quarter.

## Questions people ask before hiring an SEO company

### How much should SEO cost for a small business?

Ongoing SEO is a monthly retainer, scoped to how competitive your market is and how much content it takes to match the sites already ranking. A one off fix, such as rebuilding the money pages on an existing site, is a fixed project price. We give you a number after one call and it does not change mid engagement.

### How long does SEO take to work?

Three to six months for the first real movement, and closer to twelve for the compounding to show. A page we change today takes two to three months to judge. Anyone who promises page one in two weeks either does not know what they are talking about or is lying.

### Do you guarantee first page rankings?

No, and nobody can. What we guarantee is the work: the right pages, built the right way, in the right order, with the data to show what moved. We have taken sites to page one and into the top three. It takes months of consistent work, not a trick.

### Is SEO dead now that people ask AI?

No. AI answers are assembled from pages that rank, so the sites winning in ChatGPT are the sites winning on Google. What changed is the format: the answer has to be at the top of the page in plain text, not buried under a hero video. We build every page that way, and we track AI referrals separately so you can see them grow.
