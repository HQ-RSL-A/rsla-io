## Who this is for

You started the business. You also answer the phone, send the quotes, chase the deposits, and remember who said "call me in March." Leads live in your text messages, your email, a notebook in the truck, and your head.

It works until it does not. A lead texts while you are on a job, you mean to reply, and by the time you do they have hired someone else. Nobody dropped the ball on purpose. There was no system to catch it.

A CRM is that system. Ours is built on [GoHighLevel](https://www.gohighlevel.com), because for a one person or small team service business it replaces four or five separate subscriptions with one, and because we know it well enough to set it up in days instead of months.

## What a GoHighLevel expert sets up

Most people buy GoHighLevel and use a tenth of it, because the setup is the hard part. Here is what a full setup includes when we do it.

A pipeline that mirrors how you sell: new lead, contacted, quote sent, booked, done, and whatever sits between. You drag a card, the next action fires. Forms and a phone number that capture every enquiry into the same place, whether it came from the website, a text, a Facebook ad, or a missed call.

Follow up sequences that run on their own. A new lead gets a reply within a minute, then a text the next morning, then an email, then a call reminder for you, until they book or opt out. Our guide to [automated lead follow up in GoHighLevel](/blog/gohighlevel-lead-follow-up-automation) shows the exact sequence we start from.

A calendar with online booking, so "when can you come out" becomes a link. Estimate and appointment reminders so people show up. Review requests after the job. And the dashboard that shows how many leads came in this month, from where, and how many became customers.

## What you get access to on day one

One login with everything in it. Contacts, with every text, email, and call recorded against the person. A conversations inbox that pulls your SMS, email, Facebook and Instagram messages, and Google Business messages into a single thread per customer. The pipeline board. The calendar. The automation builder. A reputation tab that tracks your Google reviews and sends the requests. Reporting that answers "where did this month's customers come from" without a spreadsheet.

And the mobile app, which is the version you will use most.

## Built for the phone, not just the laptop

You are not at a desk. So the setup assumes the phone comes first. New lead comes in, your phone buzzes, you tap once to call. Add a note by talking. Move the card with a thumb. The laptop view is there for the deep work at the end of the day, and both show the same thing.

Where it gets interesting is combining the CRM with our [automations](/services/automations). Send a voice note describing the customer you just met, and an AI agent asks the two questions it needs, then creates the lead, tags it, and starts the follow up. You never open a form.

## A week with the system running

Monday, a lead comes in from the website at 7:40am while you are driving. The reply goes out at 7:41 with your name on it. Your phone shows the card. You call at lunch, quote on the spot, and the quote goes out from the app with a follow up already queued for Thursday.

Wednesday, a customer from last spring gets the rebooking text you never had to remember to send, and books online. Thursday, the Monday lead has not replied, so the second message goes out on its own. Friday, the job you finished yesterday triggers a review request, and the review lands on your Google listing over the weekend.

Sunday night you open the dashboard once. Eleven leads this month, six from Google, four booked, two still in follow up. That is the whole week of admin, and you typed almost none of it.

A coach or consultant gets a discovery call funnel with the qualifying questions built in, so the calendar only fills with people who fit. Same platform, different pipeline. The build starts from how you actually sell, not from a template.

## Why GoHighLevel and not HubSpot or Salesforce

HubSpot and Salesforce are built for sales teams with managers. GoHighLevel is built for the business where the owner is the sales team. It has the CRM, the texting, the email, the calendar, the forms, the phone number, and the automation builder in one login, at a monthly price that a one person business can justify. We wrote a full [GoHighLevel vs HubSpot comparison](/blog/gohighlevel-vs-hubspot-comparison) and a plain breakdown of [what GoHighLevel costs](/blog/go-high-level-pricing), including the parts the pricing page leaves out.

If your business genuinely needs Salesforce, we will tell you, and we will not be the ones to build it.

## Moving from spreadsheets or another CRM

Most of our setups start with a spreadsheet, a phone full of contacts, or a HubSpot account nobody logs into. We import what is clean, fix what is not, tag everything so you can find it, and rebuild the automations rather than copying the ones that never worked. Clients have come to us from HubSpot, Keap, Jobber, and plain Google Sheets. Migration is part of the setup price, not an extra.

## What we need from you to start

Access to whatever holds your contacts today. Your business phone number, or the go ahead to set up a new tracked one. Your calendar. The forms on your website, or the website itself if we are building that too. Twenty minutes of you walking through how a lead becomes a customer, out loud. That last one matters most; the pipeline is built from it.

## How a CRM setup runs

1. A free 30 minute call. You walk us through how a lead reaches you today and what happens after. We map the leaks.
2. A proposal with the pipeline design, the automations we will build, and a fixed price.
3. We build the account, import your contacts, connect your number and forms, and run test leads through every sequence with you watching.
4. A training session for you and anyone who touches leads, then handover. Monthly management is available if you would rather not maintain it.

## Questions people ask before hiring a GoHighLevel expert

### What does a GoHighLevel expert do that I cannot do myself?

Set it up right the first time. The platform can do almost anything, which is the problem: a first time user builds one pipeline, three half finished workflows, and gives up. We have built accounts for salons, chauffeur companies, agencies, contractors, and nonprofits, so the structure, the automations, and the integrations are done in days and they match how you work.

### What does a GoHighLevel setup include?

Full account configuration, pipeline design, lead capture forms and phone number, automated follow up sequences, calendar and booking, reminders, review requests, reporting, and a training session. We build it around your sales process rather than a generic template, and we import your existing contacts as part of it.

### Can you migrate us from another CRM?

Yes. HubSpot, Salesforce, Keap, Jobber, and spreadsheets are the usual starting points. How long it takes depends on how clean the records are, and we handle the whole process including the cleanup.

### What does it cost, and what about the software itself?

The setup is a fixed project price quoted after the first call. GoHighLevel itself is a monthly subscription you pay separately, and our [pricing breakdown](/blog/go-high-level-pricing) lays out the plans. Keeping us on afterwards to maintain and extend the system is a monthly retainer, and it is optional.
